# flexfare wiki

flexfare is a flight search site built around two ideas: **cities, not airports**, and **weeks, not dates**. You pick where and which weeks, and an AI reads the real fares and tells you which flight is the best choice, and why.

- **Live site:** https://flexfare.vercel.app
- **Source:** https://github.com/shankar-sachin/flexfare
