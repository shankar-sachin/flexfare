# FAQ

**Can I book on flexfare?** No. It finds flights and sends you to the airline or a booking site.

**Are the prices real?** They come from real fare data, but they're estimates until you check out.

**Why do I need an account?** Searches cost real money (AI and fare lookups), so free accounts get a fixed number a day.

**Why is the AI sometimes missing?** If it fails twice, you get a score-based ranking, marked as such, and it doesn't count against your searches.

**How do I report a security problem?** Privately, through the repository's Security tab. See SECURITY.md.
