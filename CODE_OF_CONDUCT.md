# Code of conduct

flexfare is a small project, and I want contributing to it to be a good experience for everyone.

## What I expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear instead of guessing the worst.
- Respect other people's time: keep pull requests focused and discussions on topic.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Spam, or anything that is clearly meant to disrupt the project.

## What happens

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented. I may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who don't follow these rules.

To report a problem, use the private reporting form in the [Security tab](https://github.com/shankar-sachin/flexfare/security/advisories/new) and say it's a conduct report, or contact [@shankar-sachin](https://github.com/shankar-sachin) on GitHub.
